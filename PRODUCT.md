# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two audiences. Premium riders (executives, guests, high-touch clients) book chauffeur-driven rides through the public site; they expect the booking flow to feel as composed as the service itself. Internal operations staff live in the admin dashboard daily: managing bookings, assigning chauffeurs, and watching the calendar. The ops team's context is sustained, task-dense work; speed of scanning and confidence in state matter more than spectacle.

## Product Purpose

GoldRidris a premium chauffeur service platform: a public booking surface plus an operations admin (bookings, chauffeurs, calendar, dashboard). Success means riders book without friction and ops staff can run the day's fleet from one screen without second-guessing what's confirmed, assigned, or pending.

## Brand Personality

Discreet, precise, luxurious. Old-money chauffeur service energy: quiet confidence, sharp edges (the design system uses zero border radius deliberately), gold used sparingly as a mark of quality rather than decoration. The interface should feel like a well-tailored suit, not a showroom.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS admin**: cookie-cutter shadcn dashboards, hero-metric stat cards with gradient accents, identical card grids. The admin should feel purpose-built for fleet operations.
- **Flashy gold-on-black luxe**: casino/nightclub luxury with heavy gold gradients, glossy effects, and ornamental excess. Gold is an accent earned through restraint.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint is the luxury.** Gold appears rarely and deliberately; neutrals carry the surface. If a screen feels decorated, it's wrong.
2. **Sharp by design.** The zero-radius system is identity, not omission. Keep edges crisp; avoid softening into generic rounded UI.
3. **State at a glance.** Ops staff scan constantly: booking status, chauffeur assignment, schedule conflicts must read instantly without opening anything.
4. **Typography does the talking.** Epilogue for working text, the wide Engravers face for moments of identity. Hierarchy through scale and weight, not boxes and borders.
5. **Calm under load.** Dense days should not produce dense-feeling screens; rhythm and whitespace keep the admin composed even when the calendar is full.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Follow sensible defaults: readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion respected.
